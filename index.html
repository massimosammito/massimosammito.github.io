Approach

A scientist who builds products.
Not a lab, not a black box.

I started by writing crystallographic phasing algorithms, then designed antibodies with AI, then led the product organisations that put AI in front of scientists. That path is the job: understanding the science well enough to know what matters, and the AI well enough to know what is real.

Set the strategy

Vision and roadmaps for AI portfolios — today an integrated agentic AI ecosystem across the design-make-test-learn cycle at Recursion; before that the Biologics Discovery Platform at AstraZeneca.

Translate across teams

Turning biological needs into technical roadmaps, and model results into decisions — between wet-lab and computational teams, AI research and discovery, science and the business.

Prototype to de-risk

I build working prototypes with Claude Code and Codex — spec first, then agents — so an idea becomes something concrete before a team commits to it.

Lead product teams
