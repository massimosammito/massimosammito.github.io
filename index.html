About

I lead AI product organisations in biotech and pharma. Over the past decade I've moved from writing crystallographic phasing algorithms to defining and shipping AI platforms that scientists actually use — most recently a biologics discovery platform serving 200+ scientists at AstraZeneca, and now agentic products for hit identification and molecular design at Recursion. I care about the unglamorous half of AI in drug discovery: governance, data strategy, vendor economics, and getting models out of notebooks and into the pipeline.
